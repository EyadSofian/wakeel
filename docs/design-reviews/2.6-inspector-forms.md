Fixes made:
- Hint rewritten without backticks: "An expression where value is the answer; when it is false, the question is asked again."
- Ask labels its main field "Question" (`MessageEditor` `textLabel`).
- An invalid field keeps its red border while focused (`TemplateInput`, `ExpressionInput`).
- The preview follows the draft of every top-level field as it is typed (`onDraft`).
- The shot selector opens card rows only (`[data-card] > div:first-child`).

## Behaviour found while reviewing (fixed, not visual)

- Typing "Log" in the card menu chose Condition, because the search matched the group name "Logic". Results are now ranked: start of the name, then anywhere in the name, then description or group, and shown as one list while searching.
- `Esc` on the canvas with a card open cleared the whole selection; it now goes up one level, to the node, as in the inspector (§5.2).
- During the 120 ms cross-fade, the view that is leaving stayed focusable: an edit typed in that moment went into it and was lost. The leaving view is now `inert` and hidden from assistive technology.
